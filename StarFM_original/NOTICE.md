# STARFM source: origin and licence

The files in this directory are the **unmodified** STARFM program, version 1.2.2, developed by Dr. Feng Gao
(initially at NASA Goddard Space Flight Center; now maintained at the USDA-ARS Hydrology and Remote Sensing
Laboratory, Beltsville, MD). The compiled executable and the large example datasets of the original package
are not included; build the program with `make -C StarFM_original/source`.

- Official download: https://www.ars.usda.gov/research/software/download/?softwareid=432&modecode=80-42-05-10
- Catalogue record (Agricultural Research Service, U.S. Department of Agriculture):
  https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/starfm
- Licence stated in that record: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The MIT licence of this repository does not apply to these files.

Please cite STARFM as:

Gao F, Masek J, Schwaller M, Hall F (2006) On the blending of the Landsat and MODIS surface reflectance:
predicting daily Landsat surface reflectance. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing
44(8):2207–2218. https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2006.872081
